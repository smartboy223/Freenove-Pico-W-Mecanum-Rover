# Third-party notices

The hardware pin assignments, matrix patterns and kit examples originate from [Freenove's Pico car resources](https://github.com/Freenove/Freenove_4WD_Car_Kit_for_Raspberry_Pi_Pico). Original kit downloads remain in the local workspace and are excluded from the clean repository export.

Required libraries are vendored without removing their license files:

| Library | Original license file | Upstream |
|---|---|---|
| Adafruit NeoPixel | [COPYING](project-libraries/Adafruit_NeoPixel/COPYING) | https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_NeoPixel |
| Arduino-IRremote | [LICENSE](project-libraries/Arduino-IRremote-master/LICENSE) | https://github.com/Arduino-IRremote/Arduino-IRremote |

Arduino-Pico and its dependencies are installed separately through Arduino CLI and retain their upstream licenses. These notices do not change the license of any third-party code. A license for the project's own source has not been selected.
