Head orientation & clearance

Missing echoes trigger short turns to inspect other headings when recovery is enabled. Confirmed close echoes block turning. A brief retreat can use recently traveled space; recovery never reverses without recent forward history. Roaming brakes at 45 cm and resumes with 55 cm clearance. Turns are timed, with no angle tracking. The rear and corners remain unsensed—use an open, level area away from stairs.

📶 Connection options

Checking connection…

Join Freenove-Rover with no password. Tap the phone’s “Sign in to network” prompt, or open http://192.168.4.1/. Stay connected if it says there is no internet.

🏠 Join home Wi-Fi

Use a 2.4 GHz network. Successful settings are saved on the car; failed attempts keep the previous network.

The car stops before switching networks.

After joining, connect your phone to the same home Wi-Fi and open freenove-car.local or the car’s LAN IP. If joining fails, reconnect to Freenove-Rover after about 30 seconds.
